Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Milez Ahead provides chauffeured executive travel — airport transfers and private trips with a professional driver. We are a service, not a retailer: no physical goods are sold or shipped, so no shipping applies. "Delivery" of the service is the completed trip. This policy sets out how cancellations and refunds are handled.

Booking & payment

Trips are booked in advance through our booking channels. Private trips are paid by the client; where a trip is covered under a client's insurance benefit, it is settled directly with the insurer and is not charged to the client. Card payments (including optional tips and any agreed extras) are processed securely through Paystack.

Cancellations

  • Free cancellation — a booking may be cancelled at no charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled pickup time.
  • Late cancellation — a cancellation made within 2 hours of pickup, or a no-show at the pickup point, may incur a cancellation fee. This covers the driver and vehicle already allocated to the trip.
  • To cancel, contact Milez Ahead through the same channel used to book, or email support@milezahead.co.za.

Refunds

  • Where a trip paid by card is cancelled within the free-cancellation window, the full amount is refunded to the original card used for payment.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days, depending on the client's bank. We do not retain any portion of a valid refund.
  • If a confirmed trip cannot be fulfilled by Milez Ahead, the client is offered a re-arrangement or a full refund of any amount paid.

Insurance-covered trips

Trips provided under a client's insurance benefit are reconciled directly with the member's insurer. As these trips are not charged to the client, no client refund arises.

Tips

Tips are entirely voluntary and are paid in full to the driver. Because a tip is a discretionary gratuity for service already rendered, tips are non-refundable once paid.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a revised "last updated" date above.

Contact

For any cancellation or refund request, contact Milez Ahead dispatch or email support@milezahead.co.za.
