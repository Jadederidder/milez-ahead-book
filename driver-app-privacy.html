Milez Ahead Driver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

The Milez Ahead Driver app ("the app") is provided by Milez Ahead for use by its authorised drivers. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Location — precise GPS, including in the background only while you are on shift or on an active trip, so the control room can monitor the vehicle, guide you to pickups, and confirm arrivals. Tracking stops when you go off shift.
  • Document photos — images you capture or select of your licence, PrDP, ID, and vehicle disc, for driver verification and compliance.
  • Account & profile — your mobile number (used with an SMS one-time code to sign you in), name, profile photo, languages, and banking details you enter to be paid.
  • Trip & operational data — trips, statuses, messages with the control room, earnings, and expenses tied to your driver record.

How we use it

To operate the driver service: dispatch and monitor trips, route you to pickups, communicate with you, verify your documents, and pay you. We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Where it goes

Data is sent over an encrypted connection to our backend (hosted on Supabase, acting as our processor) and is accessible only to authorised Milez Ahead personnel under access controls. Push notifications are delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service.

Retention & your rights

We keep data for as long as you are an active driver and as required for operational, legal, and tax purposes. To access, correct, or delete your data, contact the Milez Ahead control room.

Permissions

You can change location, camera, photo, and notification permissions at any time in your device settings; some app functions will not work without them.
