MILEZ AHEAD DRIVER

Privacy Policy

[Last updated: 2026-07-29]

Milez Ahead Driver ("the app") is provided by Milez Ahead for use by its authorised drivers. It is a private fleet tool for vetted Milez Ahead chauffeurs — not a public, consumer ride-hailing app. This policy explains what the app collects, why it collects it, and how the information is protected.

Information we collect

  • Location — precise GPS location, including in the background only while you are on shift or on an active trip, so the control room can monitor the vehicle, guide you to pickups, and confirm arrivals. Location sharing stops when you go off shift.
  • Document photos — images you capture or select of your driver's licence, PrDP, ID, and vehicle disc, used for driver verification and compliance.
  • Account & profile details — your mobile number (used with an SMS one-time code to sign you in), your name, profile photo, languages, and the banking details you enter in order to be paid.
  • Trip & operational data — trips, trip statuses, messages exchanged with the control room, earnings, and expenses tied to your driver record.

How we use your information

We use the information solely to operate the driver service: to dispatch and monitor trips, route you to pickups, communicate with you, verify your documents, and pay you. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Where your information goes

Your data is transmitted over an encrypted connection to our backend, which is hosted on Supabase acting as our data processor. It is accessible only to authorised Milez Ahead personnel under access controls.

Device permissions

The app requests only the permissions its features require:

  • Location (including background location) — live position while on shift and during an active trip.
  • Camera and photo library — to capture or choose photos of your compliance documents.
  • Notifications — to alert you to new trip assignments and messages from the control room.

You can change any of these permissions at any time in your device settings. Some app functions will not work without them.

Data retention & your rights

We retain your data for as long as you are an active driver, and thereafter for as long as required for operational, legal, and tax purposes. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting Milez Ahead dispatch or using the contact details below.

How to delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your Milez Ahead Driver account and associated personal data at any time. To request deletion:

  1. Email info@milezahead.co.za with the subject line “Delete my data”, or contact Milez Ahead dispatch.
  2. Include the full name and the mobile number registered on your driver account so we can verify the request.
  3. We action verified deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email once complete.

What is deleted: your driver profile and photo, contact details, banking details, uploaded documents (licence, PrDP, ID, vehicle disc), in-app messages, and location history.

What we may keep: trip and financial records that we are legally required to retain for tax, accounting, and regulatory purposes. These are kept for the period required by South African law (generally up to 5 years) and then deleted. This retained data is not used for any other purpose.

Children

The app is intended only for authorised adult drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact Milez Ahead dispatch or email info@milezahead.co.za.
